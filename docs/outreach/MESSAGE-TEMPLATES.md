# Message Templates — Outreach Pipeline

> Warm, personal, non-spammy. The templates below are **starting points** — every send must be personalised with at least one specific detail from the prospect's profile. LinkedIn fingerprints repeated message bodies; identical text to 30 people is a fast lane to a ban.
>
> All templates have **UK** and **ME** variants where the positioning differs. Use placeholders `{FIRST_NAME}`, `{KID_HINT}`, `{CITY}`, `{POSITIVE_SIGNAL}` etc. Replace before sending.

---

## Step 2 — Connection request notes (300 character LinkedIn limit)

### Variant A — UK, professional → professional (default)

```
Hi {FIRST_NAME} — really enjoyed your post on {TOPIC}. I run a small
UK online tutoring service (PGCE-qualified, KS2–A-Level) and I keep
finding interesting people in {INDUSTRY} on here. Happy to connect
either way — no pitch.
```

### Variant B — UK, when family signal is strong

```
Hi {FIRST_NAME} — saw you mentioned {KID_HINT} in your bio. I run a
science tutoring service for KS2–A-Level (all PGCE-qualified) and
collect notes from other UK parents on what's actually working. Mind
if we connect?
```

### Variant C — ME, British education angle

```
Hi {FIRST_NAME} — I lead a UK online tutoring team (every tutor
PGCE-qualified, British curriculum specialists). Saw your {ALUMNI_OR_CITY}
background and thought you'd be a good person to know in the British-
education community here. Happy to connect.
```

**Rules for connection notes:**

- Never lead with "I have a service for you". Lead with *them*.
- One concrete reference from their profile — a recent post, their employer, their school, their city, their alumni.
- "No pitch" or "Happy to connect either way" lowers the perceived ask and lifts accept rate ~30%.
- Don't write "Hope this finds you well" — flagged as automated.

## Step 3a — First DM after acceptance (within 24h of accept)

### Variant A — UK, default

```
Thanks for connecting, {FIRST_NAME}! Quick context on me — I'm
{NAME}, running Dr Tutor, a UK online tutoring service. All our
tutors are PGCE-qualified and we focus on KS2 → A-Level, mostly
sciences and maths.

I'm not pitching anything — just curious what's keeping UK parents
busiest these days. Anything happening in your family's school world
that's been particularly rewarding or particularly stressful?
```

### Variant B — ME, premium angle

```
Thanks for connecting, {FIRST_NAME}! Quick context — I'm {NAME}, I
run Dr Tutor, a UK-based online tutoring service. Every one of our
tutors is PGCE-qualified, and we work with quite a few families in
{CITY/COUNTRY} who are preparing their children for British school
entry, A-Levels, or UK university admission.

Genuinely curious — what's your family's path looking like? Any of
the children at the stage where UK education becomes a real question
for you?
```

**Rules for first DM:**

- Reaffirm value (PGCE, KS2–A-Level) but don't sell.
- End with an open, genuine question. Make it easy to reply.
- Length: 2 paragraphs max. Long DMs convert worse.

## Step 3b — If no reply after 4 days, one follow-up nudge

```
Hey {FIRST_NAME} — quick nudge in case my earlier message got buried.

No worries either way; if it's not the right time, that's fine. If
you ever do want to chat about tutoring at any stage (or you know
another parent who might), I'm easy to find.

— {NAME}
```

**Send this once and only once.** Two follow-ups = annoying; three = unsolicited.

## Step 4 — The consent ask (after 2–4 message exchange)

This is the legal bridge. It must be **explicit**. The wording matters — keep a screenshot of their reply as proof.

### Variant A — Soft, conversational

```
Sounds like {RELEVANT_THING_THEY_SAID}. We have a free assessment
session and a short overview I can send by email — just a single
PDF with how our tutoring works, pricing tiers, and the booking
link.

OK if I send it to {THEIR_EMAIL_IF_GUESSED} — or which is your best
email?
```

### Variant B — Direct ask

```
Mind if I email you a one-pager? Covers how the sessions run, who
our tutors are, and our pricing. No automated nonsense — I'll
write it personally to you.

What's the best email for you?
```

**What counts as consent:**

- ✅ "Yes, send it to me at me@example.com"
- ✅ "Sure, my email is X"
- ✅ "Please do"
- ❌ A 👍 emoji alone (ambiguous — get explicit text)
- ❌ Silence (definitely not consent)

When you have the email, **screenshot the DM exchange** and attach it to the prospect's HubSpot record in the `dm_consent_screenshot` field. That screenshot is your defence if the prospect later forgets they consented and complains.

**Email requirements (PECR-mandatory):**

The legal footer block at the bottom is not optional. Do not strip it for brevity.

## Reply to "remove" / unsubscribe

Within 24 hours of receiving a remove request:

1. Reply once:

   ```
   Done — your details have been removed. Best of luck with everything.
   — {NAME}, Dr Tutor
   ```

2. Delete the HubSpot record (don't just mark unsubscribed — full erasure).
3. Remove from any Instantly campaigns.
4. Log the date in a `removed.csv` audit file (just date + reason; no PII).

---

## What never to do

- ❌ Send the same message body to 10+ prospects (LinkedIn fingerprint detection).
- ❌ Use ChatGPT to generate personalisation tokens *en masse* without reading them. Bad personalisation is worse than no personalisation.
- ❌ Pitch in the connection note.
- ❌ Send the permissioned email before you have explicit DM consent.
- ❌ Forget the legal footer block.
- ❌ Pretend to be a colleague / VA when it's you. Honesty compounds.

## A/B variants for iteration

Once you have ~50 connection requests out, A/B the connection notes:

- Variant 1: opening with "Hi {NAME}" vs "Hey {NAME}" (informal vs warm).
- Variant 2: "no pitch" line vs no disclaimer.
- Variant 3: reference recent post vs reference profile (career history).

Log the accept rate per variant in the daily tracker. After 30 days, retire the loser, evolve the winner.
