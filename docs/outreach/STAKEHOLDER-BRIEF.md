## What we're building, in one sentence

A relationship-first LinkedIn-led outreach pipeline that helps Dr Tutor find affluent UK and Middle East parents, build trust with them, and convert that trust into signed students — without spamming anyone, without breaking UK law, and without losing the personal touch that makes our brand premium.

## Why now

Dr Tutor's website handles inbound demand — parents who already know they need a tutor and search for one. But every premium tutoring business eventually needs an outbound channel to find the right families *before* they search. Outbound, done well, is what separates a £50k/year tutoring practice from a £500k/year one.

## The system in five steps

1. **Research** — find profiles of parents who likely fit our ideal customer
2. **Connect** — send a personal LinkedIn connection request, never a pitch
3. **Converse** — listen, build trust over a few messages
4. **Consent** — they say "send me info" in DM (this is the legal bridge)
5. **Convert** — permissioned email with the free assessment link → trial → signed student

Email is **never** sent without the prospect's explicit consent in a LinkedIn DM. That single rule is what keeps this system legal under UK GDPR + PECR and keeps our brand premium rather than spammy.

## Who we target

| Segment | Profile | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|
| **UK** | Affluent senior professionals (35–55), finance / law / medicine / tech / academia, with school-age children, UK-wide | High standards for their child's education, value time, distrust marketplace tutors, willing to pay £40–£70/hour for the right specialist |
| **Middle East** | UAE, Qatar, Saudi, Kuwait families — typically UK-university alumni or British international school families | Want British accent + UK curriculum alignment from day one; preparing kids for UK boarding entry, GCSE, A-Level, Oxbridge / Russell Group; less price-sensitive |

## A typical day — 75 minutes

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| Research | 15 min | Find 25 fresh profiles matching our ICP filters |
| Connection sends | 15 min | Send 15 personal connection requests (10 UK, 5 ME) |
| Conversation tending | 15 min | Reply to anyone who messaged back — same-day reply doubles conversion |
| Follow-ups | 10 min | First DM to anyone who accepted ≥ 2 days ago |
| Email layer | 15 min | Send permissioned emails to anyone who consented in DM |
| Tracker update | 5 min | Log the day's numbers |

Six days a week. Sustainable for one founder. By Phase 3 (month 6+), this drops to 15–20 min/day with a VA in the research seat.

## Tailored messages — never generic

We never paste the same text into 50 chats. LinkedIn fingerprints repeated bodies; recipients can feel it; conversion collapses; the account eventually gets restricted. Every message is built around the specific person we're sending to.

### What we don't send

> "Hi, I run a great tutoring service for your child. We offer premium online lessons at competitive rates. Reply for more info."

### What we do send (UK example)

> "Hi Sarah — saw your post about Henry starting Year 9 next term, congrats! I run a small UK PGCE-qualified tutoring service and a lot of our families come to us at exactly that stage when GCSE pressure starts to ramp up. Happy to connect either way — no pitch."

### What we do send (Middle East example)

> "Hi Ahmed — noticed your LSE background and that you're based in Dubai. I run Dr Tutor, a UK online tutoring service where every tutor is PGCE-qualified. We work with quite a few Dubai families preparing children for British boarding entry or A-Levels. Happy to share what tends to work in those applications — even if you're not in that situation, good to know each other."

The personalisation is the difference between a 5% accept rate (generic) and a 35% accept rate (tailored). It is also what makes the channel feel premium — like a thoughtful introduction, not a cold call.

## Why this is legal

Three regimes govern outreach in the UK: GDPR, PECR (electronic marketing law), and LinkedIn's own terms of service. The consent-bridge funnel above sits cleanly inside all three:

- **LinkedIn messages** between connections are governed by LinkedIn ToS only — not PECR. We can send these freely at human pace.
- **Cold email to parents** would breach PECR — so we never do it.
- **Permissioned email** after explicit DM consent is PECR-compliant — and we always screenshot the consent DM as audit proof.
- Every email carries a legal footer (DRTUTOR LTD, company number, registered address) and a clear opt-out. Removal requests are honoured within 24 hours.

The full legal frame and ICO precedent are documented separately in `docs/outreach/LEGAL-GDPR-PECR.md`.

## Consistency effects — why daily matters

Outreach is a compounding channel, not a one-time campaign. The forecast above assumes daily consistency. Here is what happens if discipline slips:

| Cadence | Outcome vs realistic forecast |
|---|---|
| 6 days/week (planned) | 100% — forecast achieved |
| 4 days/week | ~55% of forecast — conversations cool between contacts |
| 2 days/week | ~15% of forecast — most leads go cold, momentum lost |
| Inconsistent bursts (50/day for 3 days, then stop) | Channel dies — LinkedIn restricts the account |

LinkedIn rewards steady-state activity and punishes burst patterns. **Consistency is the entire competitive moat** — anyone can find the same prospects, but very few founders actually show up daily for a year.

## How we make this autonomous in the future

We will **never** fully automate the messaging layer — the manual touch is the entire reason this channel beats automated competitors. But research, scheduling, follow-up reminders, CRM updates, and analytics can become progressively more autonomous in four phases.

### Phase 1 — Manual quality (now, 30-day pilot)
Founder personally sends every message. Light tools for research only (Sales Navigator + a LinkedIn read-only MCP). 75 min/day.

### Phase 2 — Claude-assisted (months 3–6)
Add a Claude-powered assistant via Claude's task scheduler and a Chrome browser extension. The assistant:
- Drafts personalised connection notes from the profile in front of you — founder reviews + sends with one click
- Drafts initial DM replies from conversation context
- Surfaces who needs follow-up today, who's been ignored too long
- Logs every action to the CRM automatically
This cuts daily time from 75 min to ~30 min while keeping the quality bar exactly where it is.

### Phase 3 — Virtual assistant for research (months 6–12)
A VA handles the research layer: pulls profiles, drafts personalisation notes, queues them for founder review. Founder spends time only on actual conversations and trial bookings. Daily founder time drops to 15–20 min. VA cost: ~£300–£600/month — pays for itself with a single extra signed student per quarter.

### Phase 4 — Guarded automation (month 12+)
Possible future state once trust + audit trail are mature. Claude browser-based automation handles:
- Full-day research and scoring
- Queued message drafting with quality scoring
- Scheduled sends only after founder approves the day's batch
- CRM updates and follow-up reminders end-to-end
- Daily / weekly summary reports

### Critical guardrails — all phases
- **Every outbound message reviewed by a human before send.** No exceptions. Ever.
- Daily LinkedIn volume capped at 20 connection requests (we operate at 15) to stay inside LinkedIn ToS.
- Email layer never operates without a stored consent screenshot.
- Weekly audit of removed-data requests.
- Founder remains the visible identity behind every conversation — relationships are with a person, not with a brand or a bot.

The principle: **automation handles repetition; humans handle judgement.** This is the line that keeps the system alive for a decade rather than burning out in three months.

## Risks we are actively managing

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn account restriction | Manual messaging cap (≤ 20/day), varied wording, no third-party automation tools (Phantombuster, Dripify, etc. — all banned by LinkedIn) |
| UK GDPR / PECR breach | Consent funnel is non-negotiable; screenshot every DM consent; honour removal within 24h |
| Founder burnout | 6-day-per-week cap, weekly review built in, VA path at Phase 3 |
| Channel-fit failure for B2C | 30-day pilot has a five-path decision tree; if it doesn't convert at the predicted rates, tooling pivots to B2B (schools, corporate benefits) without re-architecting |
| ME jurisdictional differences | Operate to UK GDPR / PECR standard globally — strictest regime covers all others |

## Closing — why this matters

The website and SEO programme capture parents who already know they want a tutor and are searching. This outbound system reaches the parents who would love what we offer but don't know we exist yet. Most premium tutoring businesses never solve this — they wait for inbound and accept a ceiling.

Done well — quality over quantity, consistency over bursts, manual touch over automation — this channel becomes the difference between a comfortable practice and a category-leading brand. The 30-day pilot tells us whether it is the right fit for Dr Tutor. Whatever the result, the system is built to be honest, legal, and proud-of.
