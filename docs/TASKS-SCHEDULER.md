## How to run a GBP review push (plain English)

**What "GBP" means.** Google Business Profile (used to be called Google My Business). It's the listing for **Dr Tutor** that shows up:
- On the right side of Google when someone searches for your brand
- On Google Maps
- In the "local pack" (the three businesses Google shows for local-intent queries like "tutoring near me")

You already have one. According to the R&D, it currently shows **5.0 stars from 4 reviews**, in the London area, phone +44 7526 327612.

**Why reviews matter (so much).** Google ranks GBPs partly on review count, partly on review quality, partly on recency. Your **rating** (5.0) is better than MyTutor (4.3) and Tutorful (3.5) — but their **review counts** (448 and 335) are 100× yours. That's not a bad spot to be in; it just means the cheapest possible upgrade you can give your brand is more reviews. Each new genuine review:
- Lifts you in the local pack
- Adds fresh keywords to the profile (Google reads review text)
- Sends a "real business, real customers" signal to LLMs (Perplexity / ChatGPT cite businesses with strong review profiles)

**How to ask (the actual mechanics).**

1. **Get your one-click review link** — once.
   - Open your GBP at https://business.google.com (sign in as the GBP owner).
   - Click *Read reviews* → *Get more reviews*.
   - Copy the short URL (looks like `g.page/r/AbCdEf…` or `https://g.page/r/<id>/review`). That link drops anyone who clicks it straight into the review-writing screen, already logged into Google. Zero friction.

2. **Make a short list of who to ask.** Past students whose families had a good experience. Aim for **30 families** in week 1 (today's plan splits them into batches of 10 over Days 3 / 5 / 7).

3. **Send the email.** Personal, short. Template:
   ```
   Subject: Quick favour — would you leave us a Google review?

   Hi [Parent first name],

   Hope [Student first name] is doing well after our recent sessions.

   We're a small UK team and Google reviews genuinely change how
   easily other families find us. Would you be willing to spare 60
   seconds to leave one?

   The link goes straight to the review screen, you don't need to
   search for us:

   <paste your g.page/r/... link>

   Even one line about your experience would make a huge difference.

   Thanks so much,
   Aayan
   Dr Tutor
   ```

4. **Conversion expectation.** Of 30 personal emails, expect **8–12 reviews** back over 7–10 days. The personal touch is what makes it work — bulk-blast emails convert at 1–2%.

5. **Reply to every review** — including 5-stars. Google rewards engagement, and future readers see that you care.

6. **Don't ever incentivise reviews.** No discount-for-review, no "we'll send you something if…". Google's policy is clear; the penalty is a profile suspension and loss of all your reviews. Real reviews, real growth.

7. **Repeat monthly.** Set a calendar reminder — pick 10 of the most recent families each month, send the same email. Steady drip beats a one-time blast.
